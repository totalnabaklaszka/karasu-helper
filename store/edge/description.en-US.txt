Keep your Karasu queue settings, player ranks and cosmetic loadouts in one compact panel.

Karasu Helper is a free, unofficial community extension for karasu.live, made by totalnabaklaszka. It adds controls for the Karasu website and does not control Counter-Strike 2.

QUEUE AND VETO
Enable auto accept with your preferred 1–10 second delay. Set map and server ban priorities separately; the extension follows your order when it is your captain turn. You join the queue yourself. Automatic match actions are off on a fresh installation.

PLAYER RANKS
Show FACEIT level and ELO beside Karasu ELO in supported match tables. Choose Badge, Label or Minimal. Rank data comes from fields supplied by Karasu; it is not a direct live sync with FACEIT. Missing data stays clearly marked.

SERVER CONNECTION
Optionally copy a validated connect IP:port command when your own match server is ready. A changed server address can be copied again. This feature writes to the clipboard without reading its previous contents and is off by default.

SKINS AND LOADOUT STUDIO
Search the Karasu cosmetic catalog by weapon, finish or approximate colour. Doppler and Gamma Doppler variants retain their phase or gem name. Use indicative USD market-value and rarity filters, save favorites, or preview a random weapon or loadout.

Lock slots, reroll selected choices and save up to 12 named sets locally. Craft and equip for CT, T or both compatible teams after selecting an explicit action. A slot without a matching skin stays unchanged. Loadout batches run sequentially and stop on failure. These are Karasu cosmetics, not Steam items; the extension does not purchase, transfer or trade skins. Indicative market values are search filters, not crafting fees.

A COMPACT PANEL
Switch between light and dark themes. Your selected skin and equip actions stay below the scrolling catalog. Use the expanded Settings page for longer loadout batches.

PRIVACY AND PERMISSIONS
Preferences and saved sets stay on your device. There are no ads, analytics or developer-operated data collection servers. Match/player information is processed to provide the website features; cosmetic requests use your existing Karasu session and go directly to Karasu. The full privacy policy explains these flows.

Price data is bundled. Clicking Update prices can request optional access to Skinport's public API; it sends no Karasu account data. Donations through the optional Ko-fi link are voluntary and unlock no features.

Keep Karasu, the browser and your computer awake for match automation. Background assistance improves detection while the page is in the background; frozen tabs, sleep or connection loss can still interrupt it. Keep the expanded Settings page open while applying a longer loadout batch.
